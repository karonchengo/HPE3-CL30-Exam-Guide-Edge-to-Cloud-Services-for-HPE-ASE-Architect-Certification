## What Is the HPE3-CL30 Edge-to-Cloud - Services Exam?

HPE3-CL30 is a web-based badge exam associated with the Edge-to-Cloud - Services learning path. According to HPE's published exam information, it validates understanding of the coursework completed through continuous learning on HPE Tech Pro.

The exam is part of HPE's modular learning and credential framework. Its purpose is to assess knowledge acquired through the designated learning materials rather than to serve as a standalone, comprehensive architect certification exam.
